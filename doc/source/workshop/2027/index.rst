CVXPY Workshop 2027
===================

.. raw:: html

   <p class="workshop-2027-date">July 22–23, 2027 · Louvain-la-Neuve, Belgium</p>

The 2027 CVXPY workshop will be held alongside the JuMP-dev and HiGHS workshops.
Join us for two days of talks and conversations with people interested in
optimization and open source software.

Contribute a Talk
-----------------

We invite talks about CVXPY applications, extensions, or use cases in industry
and academia. Talks on related optimization projects in Python are also welcome.

**Submit by February 27, 2027.** Accepted talks will be announced by March 14, 2027.

.. raw:: html

   <a class="workshop-2027-button" href="https://forms.gle/qpaPeZ141hzoGyacA">Submit a Talk</a>

To discuss a talk idea, ask on the `CVXPY Discord <https://discord.gg/4urRQeGBCr>`_
or email cvxpydevs@gmail.com.

Attend the Workshop
-------------------

For registration, venue details, accommodation, and visa information, visit the
`shared event website <https://jump.dev/meetings/jumpdev2027/>`_.

Schedule
--------

The program includes shared invited talks and two parallel tracks of contributed
talks. CVXPY sessions are highlighted. **The schedule is subject to change.**
